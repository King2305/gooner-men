BU DELIVERY SERVICE - PRD



1\. PROBLEM

STRESS OF GOING TO BUSA, BABRITE AND OTHER SHOPS TO BUY FOOD AND OTHER PROVISIONS NOT AVAILABLE AT HOSTEL STORES.



2\. RESEARCH

• THE PEOPLE AFFECTED ARE MOSTLY STUDENTS WHOSE HOSTELS ARE FAR FROM THE CENTRAL SHOPPING AREA.

• THE MAIN SOLUTION RIGHT NOW IS KEKE (TRICYCLE) TRANSPORT TO AND FRO THESE AREAS.

• THIS SOLUTION IS INADEQUATE. IT IS COSTLY AND REQUIRES MULTIPLE TRIPS, DRIVING UP STUDENTS' EXPENSES DAILY.

• COST IS ABOUT 200 NAIRA PER TRIP FROM HOSTELS LIKE TOPAZ AND EMERALD HALL, AND EVEN FEMALE HOSTELS LIKE HAVILLAH GOLD AND NYBERG.



3\. SURVEY

THIS ISSUE HAS BEEN SPOKEN ABOUT BEFORE AND PREVIOUS SURVEYS HAVE BEEN CARRIED OUT. THE RESULT WAS CLEAR: THE DISTANCE IS TOO FAR FOR A NUMBER OF HOSTELS AND STUDENTS NEED A BETTER SOLUTION. A LOT OF STUDENTS WERE ALSO IN FAVOUR OF PAYING A SUBSCRIPTION FOR ONE.



4\. SOLUTION: BU DELIVERY SERVICE

THIS IS THE RIGHT SOLUTION BECAUSE WE CAN USE THE TRICYCLE NETWORK AND INFRASTRUCTURE THAT ALREADY EXISTS. WE HIRE THE TRICYCLE DRIVERS TO RUN DELIVERIES BETWEEN STORES (SELLERS) AND HOSTELS (BUYERS), CREATING A REWARDING REVENUE SYSTEM THROUGH A MOBILE APP. THINK UBER DELIVERIES ON A SMALL SCALE.

THE APP HAS THREE PARTIES: BUYER, SELLER AND DRIVER.

• BUYER AND SELLER COMMUNICATE TO MAKE PURCHASES AND PAYMENT.

• DRIVER AND SELLER COMMUNICATE FOR PICKUP.

• DRIVER AND BUYER COMMUNICATE FOR DELIVERY.

• THE DRIVER IS IN CONTACT WITH BOTH, AND ALL THREE CAN COMMUNICATE WITH EACH OTHER.



5\. MVP GOAL

THE FIRST VERSION IS SIMPLER, TO TEST THE WATER. IT RUNS ON A SMALL SCALE WITH A LIMITED NUMBER OF DRIVERS, AND BACKEND DATABASES HANDLING USER DATA FOR THE DRIVER, SELLER AND BUYER SECTIONS.



6\. MVP FEATURES



6.1 THREE INTERFACES

THE APP SHOWS A DIFFERENT INTERFACE DEPENDING ON THE TYPE OF ACCOUNT USED TO LOG IN: BUYER, SELLER OR DRIVER.



6.2 SECTOR ASSIGNMENT

EACH DRIVER IS ASSIGNED TO A SECTOR FOR DELIVERY. THIS PROMOTES CLARITY AND REDUCES DISCREPANCIES.



6.3 ORDER FLOW

1\. BUYER PLACES AN ORDER WITH A SELLER AND PAYS.

2\. ALL DRIVERS IN THE BUYER'S SECTOR ARE ALERTED WITH A PRIORITY LIST .

3\. THE FIRST DRIVER TO ACCEPT GETS THE ORDER.

4\. DRIVER PROCEEDS TO THE SELLER FOR PICKUP.

5\. DRIVER PROCEEDS TO THE BUYER FOR DELIVERY.

6\. SELLER AND BUYER ARE NOTIFIED AT EACH STAGE THROUGH AN ON-SCREEN TICKLIST SHOWING EVERY COMPLETED STAGE.



6.4 THERMAL BOXES

DRIVERS ARE EQUIPPED WITH THERMAL BOXES TO KEEP THE TEMPERATURE OF GOODS, ESPECIALLY FOOD, WHILE IN TRANSIT. THE BOXES ARE STORED IN THE TOP TRUNK OF THE TRICYCLE, AWAY FROM TAMPERING BY PASSENGERS.



7\. REVENUE MODEL

THE APP EARNS THROUGH EITHER A PAY PER DELIVERY MODEL OR A SUBSCRIPTION MODEL. THE MODEL CHOSEN DECIDES HOW DRIVERS ARE PAID.



7.1 PAY PER DELIVERY

DRIVERS ARE PAID PER DELIVERY MADE. THE DELIVERY FEE HAS TO BE WITHIN RANGE OF WHAT STUDENTS ALREADY SPEND ON THE ROUND TRIP TO BUSA AND SUCH, WHICH IS 400 NAIRA TO GO AND COME.

WE ARE ALSO PROVIDING CONVENIENCE. WITHOUT US, THE STUDENT HAS TO COME DOWN, GO OUT IN THE HOT SUN TO FIND KEKES, WALK AROUND BUSA TO BUY FOOD, THEN DO IT ALL OVER AGAIN TO GET BACK. FROM OUR SURVEYS, STUDENTS ARE WILLING TO PAY FOR THAT.



7.2 SUBSCRIPTION

DRIVERS ARE PUT ON SALARY. A SUBSCRIPTION SERVICE WILL BE ENOUGH TO COVER THE SALARIES, ADDING AN EXTRA SOURCE OF INCOME TO THEIR NORMAL INCOME.

SUBSCRIPTION PRICE: TO BE DECIDED.



7.3 EXTRA INCOME

WE CAN HOST ADS AND PROMOS ON THE APP, WHICH ALSO GIVES CUSTOMERS MORE REASON TO USE IT.



8\. DRIVER REVENUE PROTECTION

SECTOR ASSIGNMENT LETS DRIVERS MAKE DELIVERIES WHILE STILL TAKING NORMAL PASSENGERS TO AND FRO THEIR LOCATIONS. THEIR NORMAL REVENUE FLOW IS NOT RESTRICTED.



9\. SUCCESS STATISTICS

• NUMBER OF DELIVERIES COMPLETED PER WEEK

• AVERAGE TIME FROM ORDER TO ACCEPTANCE BY A DRIVER

• AVERAGE TIME FROM ORDER TO DELIVERY

• PERCENTAGE OF BUYERS WHO ORDER AGAIN

• DRIVER EARNINGS COMPARED TO NORMAL PASSENGER-ONLY DAYS



10\. RISKS TO AVOID

• NO DRIVER ACCEPTS AN ORDER IN A SECTOR: ALERTS CLOSE SECTORS THAT ARE AVAILABLE FOR THE DELIVERY.

• DRIVER IS BUSY WITH A PASSENGER WHEN AN ALERT COMES IN: DRIVERS SHOULD ONLY ACCEPT ORDER WHEN THEY ARE FREE AND NOT INTERRUPT RIDES MIDWAY.

• PAYMENT DISPUTES BETWEEN BUYER AND SELLER: ALL PAYMENTS WILL GO INTO A SECURE PAYMENT GATEWAY LIKE AN ESCROW WHICH WILL HOLD FUNDS INACCESSIBLE TO ANYONE AND WILL BE RELEASED TO SELLERS WHEN GOODS HAVE BEEN DELIVERED.



11\. SELLERS

THE SELLERS WILL BE RECRUITED BY US INDIVIDUALLY, HELPING US SET UP THEIR ONLINE STORES AND EXPLAIN HOW THE APP INVENTORY SYSTEM WORKS AND HOW TO ACCEPT ORDERS AND COMMUNICATE WITH THE BUYERS AND DRIVERS. THE SELLERS PAY SUBSCRIPTIONS TO SELL ON THE PLATFORM AFTER DEVELOPING A VIABLE USER BASE, WITH A FREE INTRO PERIOD BEFORE MANDATORY SUBSCRIPTION REQUIREMENTS.



12\. BUYERS

THE BUYERS SECTION IS SELF-EXPLANATORY, CONTAINING A SEARCH SYSTEM AND STORE FEED WITH ADS SECTIONS AND DEALS SECTIONS LIKE AN IDEAL ONLINE STORE, WITH USER PROFILE AND DATA SUCH AS PAYMENT INFO ETC.



13\. DRIVERS

THE DRIVERS WILL GO THROUGH A SCREENING PROCESS TO ENSURE THEY ARE UP TO THE JOB. THE WORKINGS OF THE PLATFORM WILL BE EXPLAINED TO THEM, SUCH AS HOW TO COMMUNICATE WITH BUYERS AND SELLERS AND MARK STAGES SUCH AS PICKUP, TRANSIT AND DELIVERY ON THE APP. THEN PUBLIC PROFILES WILL BE SET UP WITH DELIVERY HISTORY AND RATING, WHICH AFFECTS THINGS LIKE THE AMOUNT OF ORDERS COMING TO THEM AND BONUSES GIVEN OUT TO ACT AS INCENTIVES FOR HARD WORK.

THE WAY RATINGS WORK IS BY PUTTING DRIVERS WITH BAD RATINGS ON A LIST WHILE GIVING PRIORITY TO DRIVERS WITH GOOD RATINGS FIRST MEANING THEY GET ALERTED FIRST



14\. PAYMENT

ALL PAYMENTS ARE MADE ON THE APP INTO THE ESCROW SERVICE USING BANK TRANSFERS OR DEBIT/CREDIT CARDS.
