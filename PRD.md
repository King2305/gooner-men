BU DELIVERY SERVICE: PRD



1\. PROBLEM

STUDENTS FIND IT STRESSFUL TO GO TO BUSA, BABRITE AND OTHER SHOPS TO BUY FOOD AND OTHER PROVISIONS NOT AVAILABLE AT THE HOSTEL STORES.



2\. RESEARCH

\- THE PEOPLE AFFECTED ARE MOSTLY STUDENTS WHOSE HOSTELS ARE FAR FROM THE CENTRAL SHOPPING AREA.

\- THE MAIN SOLUTION RIGHT NOW IS KEKE (TRICYCLE) TRANSPORT TO AND FROM THESE AREAS.

\- THIS SOLUTION IS INADEQUATE. IT IS COSTLY AND REQUIRES MULTIPLE TRIPS, DRIVING UP STUDENTS' EXPENSES DAILY.

\- THE COST IS ABOUT ₦200 PER TRIP FROM HOSTELS LIKE TOPAZ AND EMERALD HALL, AND FROM FEMALE HOSTELS LIKE HAVILLAH GOLD AND NYBERG.



3\. SURVEY

THIS ISSUE HAS BEEN DISCUSSED BEFORE, AND PREVIOUS SURVEYS HAVE BEEN CARRIED OUT. THE RESULT WAS CLEAR: THE DISTANCE IS TOO FAR FOR A NUMBER OF HOSTELS, AND STUDENTS NEED A BETTER SOLUTION. MANY STUDENTS WERE ALSO IN FAVOUR OF PAYING A SUBSCRIPTION FOR ONE.



4\. SOLUTION: BU DASH (WEB APP)

THIS IS THE RIGHT SOLUTION BECAUSE WE CAN USE THE TRICYCLE NETWORK AND INFRASTRUCTURE THAT ALREADY EXISTS. WE HIRE THE TRICYCLE DRIVERS TO RUN DELIVERIES BETWEEN STORES (SELLERS) AND HOSTELS (BUYERS), CREATING A REWARDING REVENUE SYSTEM THROUGH A WEB APP. THINK UBER DELIVERIES ON A SMALL SCALE.



THE APP HAS THREE PARTIES: BUYER, SELLER AND DRIVER.

\- BUYER AND SELLER COMMUNICATE TO MAKE PURCHASES AND PAYMENT.

\- DRIVER AND SELLER COMMUNICATE FOR PICKUP.

\- DRIVER AND BUYER COMMUNICATE FOR DELIVERY.

\- THE DRIVER IS IN CONTACT WITH BOTH, AND ALL THREE CAN COMMUNICATE WITH EACH OTHER.



5\. MVP GOAL

THE FIRST VERSION IS SIMPLER, TO TEST THE WATERS. IT RUNS ON A SMALL SCALE WITH A LIMITED NUMBER OF DRIVERS, TO TEST THE VIABILITY OF THE SERVICE.



THE PILOT STARTS WITH 4 HOSTELS AND 20 DRIVERS, WITH A TARGET OF 100 DELIVERIES A WEEK.



6\. MVP FEATURES



6.1 THREE INTERFACES

THE APP SHOWS A DIFFERENT INTERFACE DEPENDING ON THE TYPE OF ACCOUNT USED TO LOG IN: BUYER, SELLER OR DRIVER. EACH RECEIVES A NOTIFICATION WHEN AN ORDER COMES IN.



\- BUYER INTERFACE: STORES ARE LISTED, AND BUYERS CAN SEARCH FOR STORES, VIEW THEIR ORDER HISTORY, AND MANAGE THEIR PROFILE AND ACCOUNT SETTINGS.

\- SELLER INTERFACE: SELLERS CAN VIEW OTHER STORES AND ALSO ACCESS THEIR OWN STORE'S BACKEND, INCLUDING NAMING, PRICING, LOCATION, STORE INVENTORY, ETC.

\- DRIVER INTERFACE: DRIVERS CAN VISIT STOREFRONTS AND ALSO CHECK THEIR DELIVERY HISTORY, THEIR RATING AND THEIR ASSIGNED AREA.



6.2 SECTOR ASSIGNMENT

EACH DRIVER IS ASSIGNED TO A SECTOR FOR DELIVERY. THIS PROMOTES CLARITY AND REDUCES DISCREPANCIES.



6.3 ORDER FLOW

1\. THE BUYER PLACES AN ORDER WITH A SELLER AND PAYS.

2\. ALL DRIVERS IN THE BUYER'S SECTOR ARE ALERTED, WITH A PRIORITY LIST.

3\. THE FIRST DRIVER TO ACCEPT GETS THE ORDER.

4\. IF NO DRIVER ACCEPTS THE ORDER WITHIN 5 MINUTES, DRIVERS IN NEARBY SECTORS ARE ALERTED.

5\. THE DRIVER PROCEEDS TO THE SELLER FOR PICKUP.

6\. THE DRIVER PROCEEDS TO THE BUYER FOR DELIVERY.

7\. THE SELLER AND BUYER ARE NOTIFIED AT EACH STAGE THROUGH AN ON-SCREEN TICK-LIST SHOWING EVERY COMPLETED STAGE.



6.4 THERMAL BOXES

DRIVERS ARE EQUIPPED WITH THERMAL BOXES TO MAINTAIN THE TEMPERATURE OF GOODS, ESPECIALLY FOOD, WHILE IN TRANSIT. THE BOXES ARE STORED IN THE TOP TRUNK OF THE TRICYCLE, AWAY FROM TAMPERING BY PASSENGERS.



7\. REVENUE MODEL

THE APP EARNS THROUGH EITHER A PAY-PER-DELIVERY MODEL OR A SUBSCRIPTION MODEL. THE MODEL CHOSEN DECIDES HOW DRIVERS ARE PAID. THE ONE TO BE USED WILL BE DECIDED.



7.1 PAY PER DELIVERY

DRIVERS ARE PAID PER DELIVERY MADE. THE DELIVERY FEE HAS TO BE WITHIN RANGE OF WHAT STUDENTS ALREADY SPEND ON THE ROUND TRIP TO BUSA AND SIMILAR PLACES, WHICH IS ₦400 TO GO AND COME BACK.



WE ARE ALSO PROVIDING CONVENIENCE. WITHOUT US, THE STUDENT HAS TO COME DOWN, GO OUT IN THE HOT SUN TO FIND KEKES, WALK AROUND BUSA TO BUY FOOD, THEN DO IT ALL OVER AGAIN TO GET BACK. FROM OUR SURVEYS, STUDENTS ARE WILLING TO PAY FOR THAT.



7.2 SUBSCRIPTION

DRIVERS ARE PUT ON SALARY. A SUBSCRIPTION SERVICE WILL BE ENOUGH TO COVER THE SALARIES, ADDING AN EXTRA SOURCE OF INCOME TO THEIR NORMAL INCOME.



SUBSCRIPTION PRICE: TO BE DECIDED.



8\. DRIVER REVENUE PROTECTION

SECTOR ASSIGNMENT LETS DRIVERS MAKE DELIVERIES WHILE STILL TAKING NORMAL PASSENGERS TO AND FROM THEIR LOCATIONS. THEIR NORMAL REVENUE FLOW IS NOT RESTRICTED.

10\. RISKS TO AVOID

\- NO DRIVER ACCEPTS AN ORDER IN A SECTOR: ALERTS GO TO NEARBY SECTORS THAT ARE AVAILABLE FOR THE DELIVERY.

\- DRIVER IS BUSY WITH A PASSENGER WHEN AN ALERT COMES IN: DRIVERS SHOULD ONLY ACCEPT ORDERS WHEN THEY ARE FREE AND SHOULD NOT INTERRUPT RIDES MIDWAY.

\- PAYMENT DISPUTES BETWEEN BUYER AND SELLER: ALL PAYMENTS GO INTO A SECURE PAYMENT GATEWAY, LIKE AN ESCROW, WHICH HOLDS FUNDS INACCESSIBLE TO ANYONE AND RELEASES THEM TO SELLERS ONCE GOODS HAVE BEEN DELIVERED.




11\. SELLERS

SELLERS WILL BE RECRUITED BY US INDIVIDUALLY. WE WILL HELP THEM SET UP THEIR ONLINE STORES AND EXPLAIN HOW THE APP'S INVENTORY SYSTEM WORKS, HOW TO ACCEPT ORDERS, AND HOW TO COMMUNICATE WITH BUYERS AND DRIVERS. SELLERS PAY A SUBSCRIPTION TO SELL ON THE PLATFORM AFTER WE HAVE DEVELOPED A VIABLE USER BASE, WITH A FREE INTRO PERIOD BEFORE THE SUBSCRIPTION BECOMES MANDATORY.



12\. BUYERS

THE BUYERS SECTION IS SELF-EXPLANATORY. IT CONTAINS A SEARCH SYSTEM AND A STORE FEED AND DEALS SECTIONS, LIKE AN IDEAL ONLINE STORE, ALONG WITH A USER PROFILE AND DATA SUCH AS PAYMENT INFO.



13\. DRIVERS

DRIVERS WILL GO THROUGH A SCREENING PROCESS TO ENSURE THEY ARE UP TO THE JOB. THE WORKINGS OF THE PLATFORM WILL BE EXPLAINED TO THEM, SUCH AS HOW TO COMMUNICATE WITH BUYERS AND SELLERS AND HOW TO MARK STAGES SUCH AS PICKUP, TRANSIT AND DELIVERY ON THE APP. PUBLIC PROFILES WILL THEN BE SET UP WITH DELIVERY HISTORY AND RATING.



RATINGS WORK BY PUTTING DRIVERS WITH BAD RATINGS LOWER ON THE LIST AND GIVING PRIORITY TO DRIVERS WITH GOOD RATINGS, MEANING THEY ARE ALERTED FIRST.



IF AN ORDER IS NOT ACCEPTED AFTER 5 MINUTES, OTHER NEARBY SECTORS ARE ALERTED.
IF NOT DRIVER ACCEPTS THE ORDER WITHIN 20MINS THE ORDER IS REJECTED AND FUNDS ARE REFUNDED



14\. PAYMENT

ALL PAYMENTS ARE MADE ON THE APP INTO THE ESCROW SERVICE USING BANK TRANSFERS OR DEBIT/CREDIT CARDS.



THIS MVP DOES NOT INCLUDE DETAILS ON FUTURE EXPANSION AND LARGER USER MODELS.

17\. USER STORIES

\- AS A STUDENT, I WANT TO CREATE AN ACCOUNT SO THAT I CAN EASILY PURCHASE FOOD AND OTHER GOODS AVAILABLE IN THE SHOPPING AREAS.

\- AS A SELLER, I WANT TO CREATE A STORE SO THAT I CAN PUSH MY GOODS TO BUYERS UNABLE TO COME PHYSICALLY TO MY STOREFRONT.

\- AS A BUYER, I WANT SMOOTH AND SEAMLESS COMMUNICATION WITH THE SELLER TO AVOID MISUNDERSTANDINGS AND ALLOW FOR QUESTIONS.

\- AS A BUYER, I WANT EASY CATEGORISATION SO THAT I CAN FIND STORES EASILY.

\- AS A SELLER, I WANT EASY MARKETING SO THAT USING THE APP IS VIABLE.

\- AS A SELLER, I WANT AN EASY STORE INVENTORY SYSTEM SO THAT I CAN ADD AND REMOVE PRODUCTS EASILY.

\- AS A SELLER, I WANT TO MARK PRODUCTS AS SOLD OUT SO THAT BUYERS CAN NO LONGER PURCHASE THEM.

\- AS A BUYER, I WANT SMOOTH COMMUNICATION WITH THE DELIVERY DRIVER TO ALLOW EASY DELIVERY.

\- AS A BUYER, I WANT DETAILED NOTIFICATIONS OF THE STAGES OF MY ORDER, FROM PICKUP TO DELIVERY, FOR EASY TRACKING.



18\. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

PROPOSED FEATURES ARE SCRUTINISED BASED ON FREQUENCY OF USE AND HOW NECESSARY THE FEATURE IS.



A FEATURE WITH A SMALL, SPECIFIC USE CASE THAT IS NOT NECESSARY FOR SMOOTH OPERATION OF THE APP WON'T BE ACCEPTED. FOR EXAMPLE, BEING ABLE TO SEE A LIVE VIEW OF A STORE IS AN UNNECESSARY FEATURE, BUT BEING ABLE TO SEE YOUR LIVE ORDER LOCATION ON A MAP OF THE SCHOOL IS A VIABLE FEATURE AS IT CAN BE USED FOR EASY TRACKING IN A HYPOTHETICAL SITUATION



19\. ASSUMPTIONS AND OPEN CRITERIA

\- SUBSCRIPTION PRICING IS NOT CONFIRMED FOR ALL SUBSCRIPTIONS.

\- DRIVER FEES OR SALARIES ARE NOT CONFIRMED.

\- THE DEVELOPMENT STACK HAS NOT BEEN DECIDED.

\- THE USER BASE IS ONLY ESTIMATED.

\- THE NUMBER OF DELIVERIES PER WEEK HAS NOT BEEN DECIDED, AND THE 100 DELIVERIES IS A TARGET AND ESTIMATE.



20\. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

\- THE APP SHOULD ALLOW BUYERS TO SELECT THE STORE THEY WANT TO BUY FROM, WHAT THEY WANT TO BUY, AND ANY CUSTOMISATION THEY WANT ON THE GOODS BOUGHT.

\- THE APP SHOULD NOTIFY SELLERS AND RIDERS ABOUT A DELIVERY OR PURCHASE REQUEST.

\- SELLERS SHOULD BE ABLE TO EDIT THE GOODS THEY HAVE IN STOCK AND THE ONES THAT ARE NO LONGER IN STOCK.

\- RIDERS SHOULD BE ABLE TO ACCEPT OR DECLINE DELIVERY REQUESTS IN CASE THEY ARE OCCUPIED WHEN THE REQUEST COMES IN.



21\. NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS



21.1 PERFORMANCE

\- CORE API ENDPOINTS MUST RESPOND IN LESS THAN 250MS.

\- INITIAL PAGE LOAD MUST BE UNDER 2.5 SECONDS ON 4G MOBILE NETWORKS.

\- THE SYSTEM MUST SUPPORT 500 CONCURRENT ACTIVE USERS WITHOUT THE ERROR RATE EXCEEDING 0.1%.



21.2 RELIABILITY

\- THE SYSTEM MUST HAVE A 99% UPTIME SLA.

\- DATABASE BACKUPS ARE AUTOMATED DAILY, WITH A RECOVERY TIME OBJECTIVE (RTO) OF UNDER 1 HOUR.



21.3 SECURITY

\- PASSWORDS ARE HASHED USING ARGON2ID.

\- ALL TRAFFIC IS ENFORCED OVER TLS 1.3.

\- ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC) IS ENFORCED ON ALL ADMIN ROUTES.



21.4 COMPATIBILITY

\- THE UI IS RESPONSIVE AND SUPPORTS CHROME, SAFARI AND EDGE ACROSS VIEWPORTS FROM 360PX (MOBILE) TO 1440PX AND ABOVE (DESKTOP).
